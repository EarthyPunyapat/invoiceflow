# InvoiceFlow

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Freelancers and small service businesses creating invoices, managing clients and tracking payment.

## Product Purpose
Make the path from an estimate to a sent invoice and a recorded payment clear and dependable.

## Operating Context
Next.js application on Vercel, PostgreSQL/Prisma, Google sign-in, Stripe Connect payments and Resend email. Users enter real financial records and need clear statuses, accurate totals and preserved drafts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Clients, invoices, estimates, public invoice links, PDF and CSV export, reminders, recurring draft generation and currency-specific reporting exist. InvoiceFlow Pro costs US$9/month after a 14-day free trial (see docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-launch-readiness.md); after the trial, creating and sending invoices requires a subscription while existing records and clients' payments stay available. Customer endorsements and business growth statistics are not established facts. Never invent them. Retain routes, ownership checks and working forms.

## Brand Commitments
InvoiceFlow remains the name. The user requested a full visual rework and approved a warm-white, dark-ink, restrained-green finance-tool direction with crisp tables and subtle motion. Work on this redesign without delegation. The user requested Emil Kowalski, Impeccable, Taste and frontend-design guidance; their upstream sources are installed under .agents/skills.

## Evidence on Hand
The actual app is the visual source. Demonstration invoice data must be labeled as example data; production workspace screens use their actual database data. No customer photography, endorsements or proven customer counts are available.

## Product Principles
- Make the next action obvious, especially before a user has clients or invoices.
- Preserve financial precision and distinguish invoice states with words as well as color.
- Keep drafts intact across secondary tasks.
- Prefer honest product demonstrations to unsupported marketing claims.
